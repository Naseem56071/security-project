About Royal Enan Security

Committed to Protecting What Matters Most to Your Enterprise

Royal Enan Security Services Private Limited (RESS) is a premier private security guard agency and facility management provider headquartered in Vidyanagar, Hyderabad. Our foundational philosophy is built on three core pillars: Vigilance, Integrity, and Professional Execution.

We operate in full accordance with Telangana Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act (PSARA) standards, delivering reliable physical security, trained manned guarding, and professional facility maintenance to commercial complexes, manufacturing units, retail hubs, and residential enclaves.

Disciplined Security Cadre

Guards undergo comprehensive behavioral training, gate management drills, visitor access control, and fire safety awareness.

Active Supervisory Patrols

Day and night field supervisors conduct unannounced post checks to maintain sharp alertness and strict adherence to post orders.

Complete Statutory Compliance

Full statutory adherence including PF, ESI, minimum wages, and comprehensive background verification for complete client assurance.
